# Mettelo Project Studio Catalogue

[Apply for a project](https://tally.so/r/eq4Wjo) · [About Mettelo](https://mettelo.com) · [Join our Discord community](https://discord.gg/xAVdGuMJ)

The **Mettelo Project Library** is the discovery layer for Mettelo production-style specialist and cross-functional projects across Data, Engineering, Data Science, AI, Business Analysis, Data Governance, BI and related disciplines.

Each project is built around a realistic organisational problem and links to a dedicated master repository containing the full brief, approved data guidance, deliverables, acceptance criteria and submission requirements.

## Interactive Catalogue

The catalogue under `docs/` supports:

- **Track filtering** — Data Analytics, Data Engineering, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Business Analysis and Cross-Functional;
- **Role filtering** — Business Analyst, Data Governance Analyst, Data Engineer, Data Analyst, BI Developer, Data Scientist, AI/Model Governance and project-specific roles;
- **Sector filtering** — individual sectors within combined labels, including Banking, NHS, Public Sector, Transport, Housing, Energy, Financial Services, Telecoms and more;
- **Keyword search** — project IDs, titles, roles, skills, tools, business problems, use cases and data sources; multiple words narrow results together.

The catalogue currently contains **45 projects** across six tracks, including **30 Cross-Functional projects** (MTL-XF-011–040).

## Business Analyst Project Studio

Projects **MTL-BA-041–045** offer five distinct BA experiences: process improvement, service discovery, functional requirements, technical integration and agile product analysis. They use synthetic evidence and conflicting stakeholder perspectives, with different deliverables and endpoints.

Read [the BA project selection guide](BUSINESS_ANALYST_PROJECT_STUDIO.md) to compare the projects. Each live catalogue card lists the key outputs and links directly to the master evidence and specific team submission checklist. Team composition is agreed with the cohort; individual delivery is supported by the master submission guidance.

## Cross-Functional Series

Projects **MTL-XF-011 to MTL-XF-040** are intentionally multidisciplinary. They combine roles such as Business Analysis, Data Governance, Data Engineering, Data Analysis, BI Development, Data Science, AI/Model Governance, Assurance and domain specialists.

The objective is to reproduce the dependencies found in real delivery teams rather than treating each project as a single-role exercise.

Projects 021–040 have **eight-week, 3–5-person simulated client briefs** in their master repositories. Each README explains the organisational setting, immediate planning need, accountable stakeholder and practical management use case. The accompanying business context and project brief specify the evidence pack and action record the team must hand over. Operational gains are proposed benefits to evaluate, not claimed results.

Projects **031–038** focus on private-sector commercial decisions; **039–040** cover vulnerability management and phishing triage. Their Studio cards include direct official dataset downloads and source-register guidance. Project 037 uses a residential energy dataset as a commercial-method prototype, and 038 classifies known faults rather than measuring a factory-wide defect rate.

## Team Delivery Model

The team size is defined by each master project. Projects 021–040 recommend **3–5 people**. Members combine complementary delivery roles, with named owners for each deliverable and a different member reviewing critical work. Other projects retain the team size specified in their master brief.

Each participating team creates its delivery repository under the **Mettelo GitHub organisation** and uses it to evidence:

- technical and analytical delivery;
- business analysis and requirements where applicable;
- data governance;
- collaboration through issues, branches and pull requests;
- documentation;
- final outputs;
- acceptance-criteria completion;
- assurance/UAT where required by the selected commission;
- technical handover;
- individual contribution.

## Who Is This For?

The Project Studio is designed for people who want evidence of capability through realistic team delivery rather than classroom exercises.

It supports professionals building experience, career changers, analysts moving into engineering/data science/AI, and people developing capability in Business Analysis, Data Governance, BI, assurance and related roles.
