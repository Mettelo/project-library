# Mettelo Project Library

Welcome to the **Mettelo Project Library**.

This repository contains a growing collection of real-world data and AI project ideas designed to help professionals build practical experience and demonstrate their capabilities.

Each project is inspired by genuine business challenges across industries, giving contributors the opportunity to solve meaningful problems while strengthening their portfolios.

## What You'll Find

- Business problem statements
- Project briefs
- Public datasets
- Project requirements
- Success criteria
- Suggested tools and technologies
- Sample solutions
- Presentation templates
- Portfolio guidance

## Project Categories

- Data Analytics
- Business Intelligence

- Artificial Intelligence
- Data Visualisation
- Forecasting
- Customer Analytics

- Healthcare Analytics
- Supply Chain Analytics
- Public Sector Analytics

## Who Is This For?

Whether you're learning, preparing for interviews, or expanding your portfolio, these projects are designed to help you gain hands-on experience with real business scenarios.
