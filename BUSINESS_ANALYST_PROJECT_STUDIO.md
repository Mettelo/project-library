# Mettelo Business Analyst Project Studio

Five projects, five different BA assignments. Choose the experience you want to practise, then use the master repository for the full commission, evidence releases, task acceptance criteria and team submission instructions.

| Project | BA direction | Business question | Main deliverables | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [MTL-BA-041 — Customer Returns & Refund Process Improvement](https://github.com/Mettelo/MTL-BA-041-Customer-Returns-Refund-Process-Transformation) | Process improvement and operational change | Northbank needs to distinguish avoidable operational delay from payment-provider timing and resolve inconsistent decisions across stores and online returns. | Baseline workbook and problem statement; AS-IS maps and root-cause analysis; Policy/control proposals and TO-BE operations; Options appraisal and business case; Operational transition, benefits plan and sponsor decision | 6 weeks |
| [MTL-BA-042 — NHS Outpatient Referral & Appointment Discovery](https://github.com/Mettelo/MTL-BA-042-NHS-Outpatient-Referral-Appointment-Transformation) | Discovery and service analysis | Westmere needs to separate administrative information, patient communication, clinical hand-off and capacity problems before committing to a portal or other solution. | Discovery brief and elicitation plan; Research synthesis and evidence gaps; Patient journey and service blueprint; Problem/opportunity assessment and evidence workbook; Prioritised needs, experiments and discovery decision | 4 weeks |
| [MTL-BA-043 — SME Lending Functional Requirements & Decision Rules](https://github.com/Mettelo/MTL-BA-043-SME-Lending-Application-Decision-Transformation) | Functional analysis and business rules | Alderbridge needs precise, consistent functional behaviour before a delivery team can design or estimate application-to-decision workflow changes. | Functional boundary and actor inventory; Functional Requirements Document (FRD); Use-case diagram and at least five detailed use cases; Decision tables, conceptual data model and state model; Traceability, boundary scenarios and functional handover | 6 weeks |
| [MTL-BA-044 — Employee Onboarding & Access Integration Analysis](https://github.com/Mettelo/MTL-BA-044-Employee-Onboarding-Access-Management-Transformation) | Technical business analysis and systems integration | Harrowgate needs authoritative employment events, reliable identifiers and accountable failure handling; ticket closure or directory acceptance alone does not establish worker readiness or complete revocation. | System context and integration boundary; Interface Requirements Specification; Source-to-target mapping and technical data dictionary; Event/sequence diagrams and recovery specification; Quality controls, integration scenarios and cutover/rollback plan | 6 weeks |
| [MTL-BA-045 — CRM & Sales Pipeline Agile Product Analysis](https://github.com/Mettelo/MTL-BA-045-CRM-Sales-Pipeline-Transformation) | Agile product analysis | Beaconbridge needs to choose the smallest useful capability for six sellers rather than treating every department request as mandatory first-release scope. | Product goal, personas and opportunity map; MVP definition and story map; Epics and 12–18 prioritised user stories with acceptance criteria; Wireframes/prototype for at least three linked interactions; Refinement log, release plan and product playback | 5 weeks |

## How to participate

1. Open the selected master README and understand the problem, role and decision before starting documents.
2. Read the task acceptance criteria and follow the cohort's evidence-release sequence. Public repositories expose all releases; controlled cohorts distribute files separately.
3. Create a separate participant/team delivery repository, verify Mettelo review access and retain meaningful contribution/review history.
4. Produce the selected project's outputs. An FRD is central to 043, while user stories are central to 045; no common full-lifecycle portfolio is required across all five.
5. Complete the project-specific checklist, create the final index and tag, and submit your repository URL through the cohort route.
